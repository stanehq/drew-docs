# Music

Play music in a voice channel.

## /music play

Play high-fidelity music from Spotify/Apple/Deezer.

| Option | Type | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `query` | Text | Yes | Song name, Spotify URL, etc |

## /music skip

Skip the current track.

## /music stop

Stop playback and clear queue.

## /music queue

Show current queue.

## /music shuffle

Shuffle the queue.

## /music autoplay

Toggle AutoPlay mode.

## /music stay

Toggle 24/7 mode.
