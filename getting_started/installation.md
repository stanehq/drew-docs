# Installation

## 1. Invite Drew

[Add Drew to your server](https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id=1441457111409103010&permissions=8&integration_type=0&scope=bot+applications.commands)

Pick your server and keep **Administrator** selected. Discord may ask you to complete a CAPTCHA.

## 2. Move Drew's role up

Open **Server Settings, Roles** and drag the **Drew** role above every role it may need to punish or assign (moderators, the verification role and so on). Discord never lets a bot act on roles above its own, even with Administrator.

## 3. Check that it works

Run `/gen ping`. If Drew answers, it is installed.

If nothing happens, see [Troubleshooting](../guides/troubleshooting.md).

Continue with [Setup](setup.md).
